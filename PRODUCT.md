# Product

CarePortal is a healthcare patient portal with three role workspaces: patient, doctor, administrator.
Synthetic demo data only. It gives no diagnosis or treatment advice and is not a validated clinical system.

## Users
- Patients: book appointments, read prescriptions and lab reports, pay invoices. Often on a phone, often anxious.
- Doctors: run a day of consultations, write prescriptions, request labs. Need speed and clarity.
- Administrators: approve doctors, manage departments, billing, audit. Need density and trust.

## Surfaces and modes
- Landing and auth pages: Persuade. Full motion, large editorial type.
- Patient, doctor, admin workspaces: Operate. Precision, scanability, quiet motion.

## Brand
Calm, confident, human. Clinical editorial: warm paper ground, deep ink, pine green, one coral action accent.
Plain words. No fear, no hype, no stock-photo smiles. No violet.

## Anti-references
Generic teal-on-navy health SaaS, stock doctor photography, glossy gradient blobs.

## Assumptions
Written from the approved redesign brief without a separate interview (no interview mechanism needed: the user approved a written plan).
